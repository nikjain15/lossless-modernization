The pattern behind the pattern

Five mistakes, not eight accidents.

Read together, the cases collapse into a short list of repeated decisions. That is the argument for the playbook: these are not bad luck, they are known failure modes with known counters.

Going live without evidence
TSB, Queensland Health and HealthCare.gov all shipped against a date rather than against proof. The counter is a parity bar and time in parallel treated as entry criteria, not aspirations.
Rebuilding instead of replacing
The FBI burned $170M on a big-bang rewrite that never deployed. The counter is incremental replacement with the legacy system as the source of truth until evidence says otherwise.
Assuming data means what you think
Lidl wrote off roughly €500M over a purchase-price versus retail-price assumption. The counter is treating semantics as a first-class workstream, before build.
Governance that cannot say "stop"
Post Office Horizon and Birmingham are failures of accountability, not code. The counter is one named owner per decision and a register that survives contact with politics.

The thesis

The hard part is epistemic.

The cloud target, the services, the pipelines: well understood, and frankly the easy part. The hard part is knowing the new system is right.

Parity first. Speed, cost, and elegance are negotiable. Correctness is not.

Decades of business logic sit in code no living person fully understands, encoding forgotten regulatory reasons and long-patched edge cases, wired to consumers who will notice if a single value drifts. The industry is converging on the same conclusion from every direction. In the AI era this matters more, not less: AI translates and documents; execution-based parity evidence certifies.

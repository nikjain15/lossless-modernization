# Sources: The Annotated Bibliography

**Exec summary.** Every factual claim in this repo traces back to an entry here. Sources are grouped by kind: books, pattern catalogs, industry frameworks, government playbooks, reports and surveys, post-mortems and inquiries, and AI-era research. Each entry gets one line on why it matters. Where a statistic circulates mainly through vendor content and cannot be traced to a primary document, it is flagged honestly, because a playbook about evidence should hold its own citations to the same bar.

## Books

- **Working Effectively with Legacy Code, Michael Feathers, 2004** [Goodreads](https://www.goodreads.com/en/book/show/44919.Working_Effectively_with_Legacy_Code), [key-points summary](https://understandlegacycode.com/blog/key-points-of-working-effectively-with-legacy-code/). The canonical text: defines legacy code as "code without tests," and gives the two load-bearing tools of this repo, seams and characterization tests.
- **Kill It with Fire, Marianne Bellotti, 2021** [No Starch Press](https://nostarch.com/kill-it-fire). The best book on the organizational half of modernization: modernize for value-add rather than fashion, and fix "the system around the system."
- **Building Evolutionary Architectures, Ford, Parsons, and Kua** [O'Reilly, ch. 2](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/building-evolutionary-architectures/9781492097532/ch02.html). Source of fitness functions: automated, executable checks that protect architectural characteristics as the system changes, the CI-native cousin of the parity harness.
- **Domain-Driven Design, Eric Evans, 2003** [ACL pattern reference at Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/architecture/patterns/anti-corruption-layer). Origin of bounded contexts and the anti-corruption layer, the pattern that stops legacy semantics from leaking into the new model.

## Pattern catalogs and essays

- **Strangler Fig Application, Martin Fowler, 2004** [martinfowler.com](https://martinfowler.com/bliki/StranglerFigApplication.html). The founding statement of incremental replacement; the backbone of [patterns/02-strangler-fig.md](patterns/02-strangler-fig.md).
- **Patterns of Legacy Displacement, Cartwright, Horn, and Lewis (Thoughtworks)** [martinfowler.com](https://martinfowler.com/articles/patterns-legacy-displacement/). The most complete modern catalog: Event Interception, Dark Launching, Divert the Flow, Transitional Architecture, and a sharp warning against blanket feature parity.
- **Branch by Abstraction, Fowler bliki** [martinfowler.com](https://martinfowler.com/bliki/BranchByAbstraction.html). How to make large in-place changes while shipping continuously, without long-lived branches.
- **Technical Debt Quadrant, Fowler bliki** [martinfowler.com](https://martinfowler.com/bliki/TechnicalDebtQuadrant.html). The deliberate/inadvertent x prudent/reckless framing that keeps "tech debt" from meaning everything and nothing.
- **Things You Should Never Do, Part I, Joel Spolsky, 2000** [joelonsoftware.com](https://www.joelonsoftware.com/2000/04/06/things-you-should-never-do-part-i/). The classic case against the big-bang rewrite: crufty-looking code embeds hard-earned corner-case knowledge.
- **Lessons from 6 Software Rewrite Stories, Herb Caudill, 2019** [Medium](https://medium.com/@herbcaudill/lessons-from-6-software-rewrite-stories-635e4c8f7c22). The honest counterpoint to Spolsky: rewrites sometimes work, and the stories show under which conditions.
- **Architecture Decision Records (Nygard format)** [adr.github.io](https://adr.github.io/), [templates](https://adr.github.io/adr-templates/). The de-facto standard for recording decisions; basis of [templates/adr.md](templates/adr.md).
- **C4 model, Simon Brown** [c4model.com](https://c4model.com/). The de-facto standard for current-state and target-state architecture diagrams.
- **Event Storming, Alberto Brandolini** [eventstorming.com](https://www.eventstorming.com/book/), [process-modelling essay](https://medium.com/@ziobrando/collaborative-process-modelling-with-eventstorming-17ed363650c0). The fastest known route from tribal knowledge to a shared domain map with hotspots and bounded contexts.
- **Wardley Maps** [wardleymaps.com](https://www.wardleymaps.com/glossary/evolution-stages). Value-chain-vs-evolution mapping; its "inertia" marker names exactly the components a modernization must confront.
- **Understand Legacy Code, Nicolas Carlo** [understandlegacycode.com](https://understandlegacycode.com/). The strongest individual practitioner brand in the niche: approval testing, the Mikado method, first-aid techniques for untested code.

## Industry frameworks

- **AWS 7Rs migration strategies** [AWS Prescriptive Guidance](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/prescriptive-guidance/latest/strategy-migration/overview.html). The current de-facto vocabulary for per-application disposition: Rehost, Relocate, Replatform, Refactor, Repurchase, Retire, Retain.
- **AWS Migration Acceleration Program (MAP)** [aws.amazon.com](https://aws.amazon.com/migration-acceleration-program/). Assess, Mobilize, Migrate: the readiness-assessment gate structure most large migrations now copy.
- **AWS Application Portfolio Assessment guide** [PDF](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/pdfs/prescriptive-guidance/latest/application-portfolio-assessment-guide/application-portfolio-assessment-guide.pdf). Directional-then-detailed business case method; basis of [templates/business-case.md](templates/business-case.md).
- **AWS cutover runbook guidance** [Prescriptive Guidance](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/prescriptive-guidance/latest/cutover-runbook/welcome.html) and [pre-cutover best practices](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/prescriptive-guidance/latest/best-practices-migration-cutover/pre-cutover-stage.html). The only major vendor that ships a literal cutover-runbook template with rollback as a mandatory section.
- **AWS large-migration wave planning** [Prescriptive Guidance](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/prescriptive-guidance/latest/large-migration-portfolio-playbook/wave-planning.html). Grouping applications into waves by dependency and similarity; basis of [templates/wave-plan.md](templates/wave-plan.md).
- **AWS Mainframe Modernization patterns** [aws.amazon.com](https://aws.amazon.com/mainframe-modernization/capabilities/). The automated-refactoring vs replatforming split, decided per application, with functional-equivalence evidence expected.
- **Azure Cloud Adoption Framework** [Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cloud-adoption-framework/overview). Strategy through Manage: the most complete governance wrapper around a migration program.
- **Google Cloud migration framework** [cloud.google.com](https://docs.cloud.google.com/architecture/migration-to-gcp-getting-started). Assess, Plan, Deploy, Optimize, with Migration Center as tooling.
- **Google Cloud Dual Run** [Google Cloud blog](https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/infrastructure-modernization/dual-run-by-google-cloud-helps-mitigate-mainframe-migration-risks). Industrial-scale parallel running as a product: mainframe and replica side by side on production transactions, outputs compared, parity certified before cutover. External validation of this repo's central method.
- **IBM Redbook SG24-8532, Mainframe Application Modernization Patterns for Hybrid Cloud, 2023** [redbooks.ibm.com](https://redbooks.ibm.com/abstracts/sg248532.html). The strongest argument for "best fit placement": modernizing on the mainframe is a legitimate outcome, not a failure.
- **Gartner 5Rs, Migrating Applications to the Cloud, 2010-2011** [gartner.com](https://www.gartner.com/en/documents/1485116). The original R taxonomy everything else evolved from.
- **Gartner TIME model** [LeanIX explainer](https://www.leanix.net/en/wiki/apm/gartner-time-model). Tolerate, Invest, Migrate, Eliminate on a business-value x technical-fit 2x2; the portfolio triage in [decide/choose-your-strategy.md](decide/choose-your-strategy.md).

## Government playbooks

- **18F De-risking Government Technology Guide, v2.0, 2024** [guides.18f.gov](https://guides.18f.gov/derisking/federal-field-guide/), [2024 update note](https://www.gsa.gov/blog/2024/09/11/a-revised-and-expanded-guide-for-derisking-government-technology-projects). The best free text on budgeting, modular contracting, and vendor oversight for large modernizations.
- **UK GDS Service Manual, Moving Away from Legacy Systems** [gov.uk](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/technology/moving-away-from-legacy-systems). Wrap legacy behind APIs, control pace with wrappers, and dark-launch: send real queries to the new system and compare answers with the old.
- **UK CDDO Legacy IT Risk Assessment Framework** [gov.uk](https://gov.uk/government/publications/guidance-on-the-legacy-it-risk-assessment-framework/guidance-on-the-legacy-it-risk-assessment-framework). Standardized RAG scoring with seven legacy indicators; basis of [templates/risk-register.md](templates/risk-register.md).
- **USDS Digital Services Playbook** [playbook.usds.gov](https://playbook.usds.gov/), [source repo](https://github.com/usds/playbook). Thirteen plays including the anti-big-bang stance: route traffic gradually and let users revert to legacy after launch.
- **DoD Software Modernization Strategy, 2022** [media.defense.gov PDF](https://media.defense.gov/2022/Feb/03/2002932833/-1/-1/1/department-of-defense-software-modernization-strategy.pdf). Software factories and DevSecOps at the scale of the largest legacy estate on earth.

## Reports and surveys

- **vFunction/Wakefield, Why App Modernization Projects Fail, 2022** [PDF](https://info.vfunction.com/hubfs/Download%20Assets/Wakefield-Report-2022-Why-App-Modernization-Projects-Fail.pdf). Key stat: 79% of modernization projects fail, averaging $1.5M and 16 months. Honesty flag: vendor-commissioned survey (vFunction sells modernization tooling); the fieldwork is Wakefield's.
- **Standish Group CHAOS reports** (no free primary source; figures widely republished, e.g. via [QuerySurge white paper](https://www.querysurge.com/resource-center/white-papers/strategic-optimization-of-enterprise-data-migration-testing)). Key stat: large IT projects run 45% over budget, deliver 56% less value, and fewer than 10% fully succeed. Honesty flag: methodology debated and primary reports paywalled; treat as directional.
- **Gartner data-migration failure figure** (widely attributed; circulates via vendor content such as the [QuerySurge white paper](https://www.querysurge.com/resource-center/white-papers/strategic-optimization-of-enterprise-data-migration-testing), alongside Bloor's ~75%-fail and 30%-cost-overrun figures). Key stat: 83% of data migrations fail or overrun. Honesty flag: no traceable primary Gartner document; always cite as "widely attributed to Gartner."
- **Deloitte 2025 mainframe skills findings** [via BizTech Magazine](https://biztechmagazine.com/article/2025/04/how-financial-services-companies-can-maintain-mainframes-cobol-experts-retire). Key stat: 79% of financial firms cite mainframe talent acquisition as a top challenge; 71% report understaffing.
- **Accenture 2025 mainframe survey (1,000 C-suite)** [via AWS blog](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/migration-and-modernization/reimagining-mainframe-applications-with-accenture-and-aws-transform/). Key stat: 90% of organizations are pursuing mainframe-to-cloud but only 19.5% of workloads have moved; 58% carry $21M-$100M in annual mainframe cost. Honesty flag: published through a vendor partnership channel.
- **Stripe, The Developer Coefficient, 2018** [PDF](https://stripe.com/files/reports/the-developer-coefficient.pdf). Key stat: developers lose 33-42% of their week to technical debt and maintenance, roughly $85B/year in opportunity cost.
- **IBM Institute for Business Value, mainframe modernization research, 2024** [IBM IBV](https://www.ibm.com/thought-leadership/institute-business-value/en-us/report/mainframe-application-modernization). Key stats: 61% of executives call generative AI important to their mainframe plans; median organization has modernized only 28% of applications yet 85%+ report satisfaction with hybrid approaches. Honesty flag: IBM sells both the mainframes and the AI.
- **GAO, IRS legacy systems (GAO-25-107611)** [PDF](https://www.gao.gov/assets/gao-25-107611.pdf). Key fact: the IRS Individual Master File is 60+ years old, the sharpest example of the cost of never finishing.
- **GAO, federal IT O&M spending (GAO-23-106821)** [gao.gov](https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-23-106821). Key stat: roughly 80% of the federal government's $100B+ annual IT budget goes to operations and maintenance of existing systems.
- **GAO, IRS modernization schedule (GAO-25-107795)** [gao.gov](https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-25-107795). Key fact: CADE was abandoned in 2009; its successor CADE-2 keeps slipping, a two-decade cautionary tale on replacement without incremental delivery.
- **Lucid survey 2025 on workflow documentation** [via Sentra](https://www.sentra.app/articles/tribal-knowledge). Key stat: only 16% of organizations say their workflows are well documented. Honesty flag: secondary summary.
- **Enboarder 2025 / IDC knowledge-loss figures** [via Kintone](https://blog.kintone.com/business-with-heart/tribal-knowledge-into-team-knowledge). Key stats: 76.6% cite institutional knowledge loss as the top offboarding concern; IDC estimates Fortune 500 firms lose ~$31.5B/year to failure to share knowledge. Honesty flag: vendor blog aggregation.
- **Microservices reversal coverage** [Fabres analysis](https://fabres.eu/blog/why-most-monolith-to-microservices-migrations-fail-part2/), [ByteIota roundup](https://byteiota.com/microservices-rollback-2026-42-return-to-monoliths/). Key stats: 73% of monolith-to-microservices migrations reported failing or producing slower systems; ~42% of adopters consolidating back toward modular monoliths. Honesty flag: blog-tier aggregation, use directionally.
- **COBOL scale figures** [Adalo summary](https://www.adalo.com/posts/b2b-reliance-legacy-coding-languages-stats). Key stat: 220B+ lines of COBOL remain in production. Honesty flag: widely repeated estimate of uncertain origin.

## Post-mortems and inquiries

- **Slaughter and May, independent review of the TSB migration, 2019** [via Tech Monitor](https://www.techmonitor.ai/leadership/digital-transformation/slaughter-and-may-tsb); [FCA fine coverage, 2022](https://www.techmonitor.ai/policy/privacy-and-data-protection/tsb-it-crash-migration-bank-fca); [data-migration detail](https://icedq.com/resources/case-studies/tsb-bank-data-migration-failure). The best-documented big-bang cutover failure in banking: 5.2M customers in one weekend, 4,424 defects open at go-live, roughly £1B total. Full case: [why-modernizations-fail/post-mortems/tsb-bank.md](why-modernizations-fail/post-mortems/tsb-bank.md).
- **Post Office Horizon scandal and statutory inquiry** [Computer Weekly explainer](https://www.computerweekly.com/feature/Post-Office-Horizon-scandal-explained-everything-you-need-to-know). Roughly 900 wrongful prosecutions built on institutional refusal to believe the system could be wrong. Full case: [post-office-horizon.md](why-modernizations-fail/post-mortems/post-office-horizon.md).
- **Queensland Health Payroll System Commission of Inquiry, 2013** [inquiry site](http://www.healthpayrollinquiry.qld.gov.au/). A $6.2M payroll project that became $1.2B+ and produced 35,000 pay anomalies; IBM barred from state contracts. Full case: [queensland-health.md](why-modernizations-fail/post-mortems/queensland-health.md).
- **California State Auditor, EDD reports 2020-128/628** [report PDF](https://www.auditor.ca.gov/pdfs/reports/2020-128.1.pdf). The cost of not modernizing: COBOL-era unemployment systems collapsing under pandemic load amid $10B+ in fraud. Full case: [california-edd.md](why-modernizations-fail/post-mortems/california-edd.md).
- **IEEE Spectrum, Who Killed the Virtual Case File?, 2005** [spectrum.ieee.org](https://spectrum.ieee.org/who-killed-the-virtual-case-file). The definitive engineering post-mortem of a $170M big-bang rewrite that shipped nothing. Full case: [fbi-virtual-case-file.md](why-modernizations-fail/post-mortems/fbi-virtual-case-file.md).
- **Computer Weekly, Lidl dumps €500M SAP project, 2018** [computerweekly.com](https://www.computerweekly.com/news/252446965/Lidl-dumps-500m-SAP-project). Semantic data-model mismatch (purchase vs retail prices) as a program-killer. Full case: [lidl-sap.md](why-modernizations-fail/post-mortems/lidl-sap.md).
- **The Register, Birmingham City Council Oracle implementation** [theregister.com](https://www.theregister.com/2026/01/29/birmingham_oracle_latest/). £19M became £144M and contributed to effective municipal bankruptcy. Full case: [birmingham-oracle.md](why-modernizations-fail/post-mortems/birmingham-oracle.md).
- **Harvard D3, The Failed Launch of HealthCare.gov** [d3.harvard.edu](https://d3.harvard.edu/platform-rctom/submission/the-failed-launch-of-www-healthcare-gov/). Sixty contracts, thirty-three companies, no integrator, and end-to-end testing compressed to weeks. Full case: [healthcare-gov.md](why-modernizations-fail/post-mortems/healthcare-gov.md).
- **Hertz v. Accenture coverage** [CMSWire](https://www.cmswire.com/information-management/10-lessons-from-hertzs-32m-web-design-lawsuit-against-accenture/). $32M paid for an undelivered website; the vendor-accountability cautionary tale.

## AI-era research and tooling

- **Exploring LLM hallucinations in code (taxonomy), 2024** [arXiv 2404.00971](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.00971). Categorizes the ways LLM-generated code goes wrong; grounding for why translation output cannot be trusted without execution evidence.
- **MatchFixAgent, semantic-equivalence detection** [arXiv PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2509.16187). Execution plus LLM judging reaches ~95% equivalence detection: strong, but not sign-off strong, which is why parity harnesses stay human-owned.
- **Berkeley EECS-2025-174, formal reasoning for LLM translation** [PDF](https://www2.eecs.berkeley.edu/Pubs/TechRpts/2025/EECS-2025-174.pdf). Argues LLM code translation needs compositional formal reasoning, not more prompting.
- **LLMs for legacy comprehension** [arXiv 2411.14971](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.14971), [arXiv 2508.19663](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.19663), [multi-agent COBOL explanation, arXiv 2507.02182](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.02182v1). The consistent academic finding: LLMs help comprehension more than conversion, which is exactly how [ai/legacy-archaeology.md](ai/legacy-archaeology.md) uses them.
- **IBM watsonx Code Assistant for Z** [announcement](https://www.ibm.com/new/announcements/ibm-watsonx-code-assistant-for-z-accelerate-the-application-lifecycle-with-generative-ai-and-automation), [IBM Research on COBOL to Java](https://research.ibm.com/blog/cobol-java-ibm-z). Gen-AI COBOL-to-Java on the Granite model; works service by service, with validation still the maturing step. Honesty flag: vendor documentation.
- **AWS Transform for mainframe, GA 2025** [announcement](https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/2025/05/aws-transform-mainframe-generally-available), [Reimagine pattern blog](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/migration-and-modernization/reimagine-your-mainframe-applications-with-agentic-ai-and-aws-transform/). Agentic analysis, documentation, refactoring, and business-rule extraction for COBOL/PL1/JCL. Honesty flag: vendor claims of years-to-months acceleration are marketing until independently measured.
- **Moderne / OpenRewrite** [docs.openrewrite.org](https://docs.openrewrite.org/), [moderne.ai](https://moderne.ai/openrewrite). Deterministic mass refactoring via Lossless Semantic Trees: same recipe, same output, every time, a guarantee LLMs cannot make.
- **Mechanical Orchard, Imogen** [platform](https://www.mechanical-orchard.com/platform), [HyperFRAME analysis](https://hyperframeresearch.com/2026/05/22/the-behavior-first-paradigm-moving-mainframe-modernization-past-llm-wishful-thinking/). Behavior-first rewriting: capture production data flows, generate code, prove parity transaction by transaction. The commercial embodiment of this repo's thesis.
- **Bloop AI, COBOL to Java** [founder interview](https://scalingdevtools.com/podcast/episodes/louis-bloop). Chose static transpilation plus AI refactoring plus correctness checks precisely because pure LLM output is not semantically trustworthy.
- **Context-window limits for mainframe estates** [DataStealth analysis](https://datastealth.io/blogs/mainframe-modernization-claude-code-cobol). Why whole-estate AI translation fails: cross-program dependencies and JCL orchestration exceed any context window, and COBOL training data is thin. Honesty flag: vendor blog, but the technical argument stands independently.

The consensus across this last group, and the position of this repo: AI translates and accelerates comprehension; execution-based parity evidence certifies. See [ai/README.md](ai/README.md).
